Celebratory six-pack cards

Three prototypes of the NBA league screen with the celebratory six-pack event card. Open one and resize the browser window — the mobile screen extends to tablet and desktop, and the photo never stretches. All assets are stored locally; colors and values come from the KDS tokens in the Figma file.

Option A — Anchored players + ambient fillThe player art keeps its natural size at every width; extra width fills with a blurred wash of the same photo. Option B — Side hero on wide screensPast 720 px the photo becomes a fixed-width side panel showing more of the players; on mobile it collapses to the top-photo layout. Option C — Player figures top leftA small fixed-size cutout of the players sits on the card's top-left corner, fading into it. No photo band at all, so nothing can ever stretch.
